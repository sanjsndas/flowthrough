    Cookies Policy

    We use a minimal set of cookies to make this journal work smoothly — and we explain exactly what each one does.

    Last updated: 1 January 2025

    In short: We use essential cookies to keep the site running, and a small number of analytics cookies to understand what readers enjoy. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can manage cookies through your browser at any time.

    1. What are cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, tablet, or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide website owners with information about how their site is being used.

    Cookies are not programs and cannot carry viruses or install malware on your device. They simply store small pieces of information that help a website function properly.

    2. How we use cookies

    Flow Through Space uses a deliberately minimal set of cookies. We group them into the following categories:

    2.1 Strictly necessary cookies

    These cookies are essential for the website to function and cannot be switched off. They are usually set in response to actions you take, such as setting your privacy preferences or filling in forms. Without these cookies, parts of the site may not work.

    2.2 Analytics cookies

    These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our journal — which articles are read most, how readers navigate between pages, and where we can improve. All data collected is aggregated and anonymised, so it cannot be used to identify you personally.

    2.3 Preference cookies

    These cookies remember choices you make (such as your preferred reading mode or language) to provide a more personalised experience. They are set only when you interact with features that require them.

    We do not use advertising cookies, social media tracking pixels, or any cookies that follow you across other websites.

    3. Cookies we set

    The table below lists the specific cookies that may be set when you visit Flow Through Space:

    Cookie name Category Purpose Duration
    fts_consent Strictly necessary Stores your cookie consent preference so we don’t ask on every visit. 12 months
    fts_session Strictly necessary Maintains your session while you browse the site (e.g., form state). Session
    _pk_id Analytics Privacy-focused analytics — used to distinguish unique visits in aggregate. 13 months
    _pk_ses Analytics Privacy-focused analytics — tracks a short visit window for aggregate statistics. 30 minutes
    fts_prefs Preference Remembers display preferences such as reading mode. 6 months

    We may also use similar technologies such as local storage (e.g., for saving form drafts). These follow the same principles as cookies and are covered by this policy.

    4. Third-party cookies

    Some pages may include embedded content from third parties — for example, images from Unsplash or embedded videos. These third parties may set their own cookies when you interact with their content.

    We do not control these cookies. We recommend checking the relevant third party’s privacy and cookie policies for more information. Where possible, we use privacy-enhanced modes that limit tracking.

    5. Your cookie choices

    When you first visit Flow Through Space, you’ll see a cookie banner where you can accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your mind at any time by:

    • Using our cookie preferences tool — accessible from the footer of every page.
    • Adjusting your browser settings — most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. The exact steps depend on your browser; check its help section for guidance.

    Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how the website functions.
